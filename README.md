# Barriers to Business Household Formalisation in Vietnam

**Role:** Team Leader | April - June 2026  
**Recognition:** Top 5 - Academic Research Competition

## Project Overview

An economic and policy research project examining structural barriers affecting Vietnam's 5.2 million business households transitioning to registered enterprises.

## Research Approach

- Applied Cost-Benefit Theory and Institutional Theory.
- Analysed government compliance and economic data.
- Compared the financial implications of business formalisation.
- Developed six evidence-based policy recommendations.

## Key Findings

The analysis estimated annual post-conversion costs of approximately VND 180–200 million, compared with VND 2.7 million under the previous lump-sum system, highlighting a substantial financial barrier to formalisation.
